### 🙋 About me

- 🔎 Most of my open source time goes into the **Python search ecosystem**: [pysolr](https://github.com/django-haystack/pysolr), [django-haystack](https://github.com/django-haystack/django-haystack), [drf-haystack](https://github.com/django-commons/drf-haystack), [meilisearch-python](https://github.com/meilisearch/meilisearch-python) and [Apache Solr](https://github.com/apache/solr) itself.
- 🧰 I enjoy the work that keeps libraries healthy: support for new Python, Django and Solr releases, migrations to `uv` and `pytest`, and hardening CI pipelines against supply chain attacks.
- 🏛️ Member of [Django Commons](https://github.com/django-commons).
- 🌾 Away from the keyboard, I'm a farmer.
